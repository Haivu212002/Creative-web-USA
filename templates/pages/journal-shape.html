How to choose a nail shape that feels like you

From softly rounded to almond: a little guide to finding your fit.

Fashion Nails editorial · AI-generated illustration

Start with your everyday routine

Think about the activities your hands do most. A shorter shape can be easier to manage if you type, work with your hands, or prefer a low-maintenance look.

Round and oval: soft and simple

Rounded edges create a gentle, familiar silhouette. Oval shapes add a little length while keeping the outline soft, depending on the length and strength of your natural nails.

Square and squoval: a clean finish

A square tip has a straighter edge. Squoval softens the corners for a look between square and oval. Ask your technician which option suits your current nails.

Almond: a little extra elegance

Almond shapes taper toward a rounded point and usually need some length. Your technician can discuss whether your natural nails or an available enhancement can support the shape.

Bring a photo, keep an open mind

A reference image helps explain your preference. The final recommendation should consider your natural nail condition, comfort, and the services actually available.
