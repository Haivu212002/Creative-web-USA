Your first nail consultation, made simple

What to bring, what to ask, and how to plan your appointment.

Fashion Nails editorial · AI-generated illustration

Bring your inspiration

Save one or two images that show the color, length, and level of detail you like. Mention whether the shape or the artwork matters most to you.

Tell us what is on your nails now

Existing gel, extensions, or other products may need removal time. Let the salon know in advance so it can advise on the right appointment.

Ask about services, time, and pricing

Confirm that your preferred service is available. Ask for an estimate and explain any budget or timing preference before your visit.

Share comfort or product concerns

Mention any relevant sensitivities and ask what products are used. A salon consultation does not replace medical advice; discuss health concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.

Confirm before you travel

An online consultation request is not an appointment confirmation. Call 717.248.4131 to confirm the service, date, time, and salon opening hours.
