Five small habits for a happier manicure

Simple ways to care for your hands between salon visits.

Fashion Nails editorial · AI-generated illustration

1. Give your hands a little moisture

Wash gently, dry thoroughly, and moisturize your hands when they feel dry. If your skin tolerates it, a small amount of cuticle oil can help keep the surrounding skin comfortable.

2. Let your nails be nails

Use a tool instead of your nails to open a can or peel a label. Small changes like these can reduce unnecessary stress on the free edge.

3. Reach for gloves

For household cleaning or long periods of washing up, gloves can reduce prolonged contact with water and cleaning products.

4. Resist picking or peeling

If polish lifts, avoid pulling it off. Contact your nail technician about appropriate removal for the product you are wearing.

5. Pay attention to changes

Stop using a product that causes irritation. For persistent pain, redness, swelling, or nail changes, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional rather than covering the problem with polish.
