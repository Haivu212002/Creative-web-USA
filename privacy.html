A little clarity
about your information.

Please read this notice before submitting a consultation request.

What the form collects

Your name, phone number, chosen service, consent, and any optional email, preferred date, time, or message. Please do not include payment information, sensitive health information, or information about other people.

How it works

Submissions are stored in a database on the website server so the site operator can review consultation requests. They are not sent automatically by email or SMS. A request is not a confirmed appointment; call 717.248.4131 for confirmation.

Your choices

You can call the salon instead of using this form. To request access to or removal of a submitted request, contact 717.248.4131 and quote your request reference. The operator should remove requests when they are no longer needed.

Other services

Google Maps is embedded on the location and home pages. Opening those pages can connect your browser to Google. Optional Google Fonts may also connect to Google. The images are stored with this website. This site does not include advertising or analytics scripts.

Before a public launch

This is a working website demo. The salon operator must confirm this notice, access controls, request handling, and a retention policy before collecting real customer details on a public deployment.
