This notice describes the features currently available on the Fashion Nails & Spa website.

Planning your visit

The visit planner uses your selected service, date, and time to display a plan in your browser. It does not submit a booking request, reserve a time, or store customer details. Please call the salon to confirm an appointment. No payment information is requested or processed on this website.

Local content drafts

The Content Studio saves website content and uploaded images in this browser’s local storage when you save a draft. Drafts are not sent to a server or automatically published, and can be removed with the Studio’s restore option or your browser’s site-data controls. Exporting JSON downloads a copy to your device. Do not put passwords, customer records, or other private information in a draft. The Studio is a local editing tool, not a protected customer database.

External links and hosting

The site links to Facebook and Google Maps. Opening those links takes you to services with their own privacy practices. The current website uses locally hosted photos and does not include advertising or analytics scripts. If the operator adds an externally hosted image, loading it connects your browser to that image host.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical information, such as request and connection logs, under its own policies. The current public pages use system fonts and local stylesheets.
